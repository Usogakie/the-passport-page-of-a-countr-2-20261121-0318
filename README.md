The passport spread of the Republic of Marenveld — a small North Sea island
republic whose capital is Port Anselm, reached by ferry and coastal steamer
only, never by air. The open spread shows the holder's data page (portrait,
machine-readable zone, valid MRZ check digits) facing a visa page: a
Drenmark visa, entry and exit stamps from Drenmark, Aurelia and the Isle of
Keth, a lighthouse-levy fee stamp, and the printed condition of issue that
reveals what kind of place Marenveld is. Every date agrees: the passport
was issued 12 March 2024 and no stamp predates it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

That copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, drawn by `draw_passport.py`) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, or `/output/entry.png` when the variable
is unset. To redraw the spread from scratch:

```sh
python3 draw_passport.py
```
